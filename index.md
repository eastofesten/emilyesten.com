Emily Esten (they/them) brings documentation, training, and coding together to help others succeed. In Emily’s varied work experience - from project lead to curator, educator to site developer - their primary focus has been shaping collaborations, training, and programming for community-oriented tech work.

Emily previously worked at Knowledge Futures and University of Pennsylvania Libraries. They currently serve as a representative on the Executive Council for the [Association for Computers and the Humanities](https://ach.org/about/officers/). Previously, they served on the Board of Directors for the [National EMP Network](https://www.nationalempnetwork.org/). In addition to their day-to-day work, Emily is a freelance site developer and arts critic.
